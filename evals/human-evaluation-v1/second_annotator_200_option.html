Recording answers

Evidence quote: copy an exact nonempty substring. “Use full original text” is optional and only fills it after you click. Entities: enter a JSON list of source terms, such as ["offline"], or explicitly choose [] if there are none. Alternatives: optional JSON lists of defensible labels, such as ["playback","downloads"] or [3,4]. Explain alternatives in the notes. Optional evidence support: supported = your selected quote supports your selected labels; partial = it supports only part; unsupported = it does not support them; unclear = you cannot tell. This is your judgment about your own quote and labels. Auditing model quote entailment is a separate task after human labels and predictions are locked. Needs review and ambiguous are independent judgments. Neither defaults to “no.” A valid form does not establish correctness.
