Severity

Intent precedence: cancellation → complaint → request → praise → unclear. Cancellation means explicitly leaving, uninstalling, cancelling, or threatening to do so. Mixed praise/criticism is a complaint unless personal departure is expressed. A desired change without a reported failure is a request. Bare boycott slogans are unclear unless they state a product complaint or personal departure.

Do not infer impact: stars, anger, or cancellation alone do not increase severity. “Bad app” is other / complaint / severity 2. “I wish there were more themes” is usability / request / severity 1. “I cannot log in at all” is access / complaint / severity 4. “Downloaded songs stop when I am offline” concerns downloads; apply severity to the explicitly reported impact.

Paid-plan boundary: “I pay, and music keeps crashing” is playback. A paid subscription that never activates is billing. A paid-plan mention alone is not billing.

Sentiment: −1 is strongly negative, −0.5 negative, 0 neutral or genuinely balanced, 0.5 positive, and 1 strongly positive. Intermediate values are allowed. Do not use stars.
